Simple and fast

How it works

Three steps that will change the way you see every price, forever.

1
Enter your salary

Gross or net, monthly, weekly or hourly. Owoda calculates your real hourly rate, accounting for holidays and commute time.

2
Enter a price

Type any amount: rent, a phone, a meal. Owoda instantly converts it into your currency, even from a foreign currency.

3
See the truth

That smartphone at 450,000 CFA costs nearly 3 months of work. That rent at 75,000 CFA, 10 days. That meal at 1,500 CFA, about an hour and a half. Decide with full knowledge.

* Example based on an average hourly rate. Your actual result depends on your own income and work hours (including commute, holidays, and non-worked paid time).

Owoda is manual by design. Automating tracking means dealing with your expenses after the fact, looking at a table that tells you what you've already done. Owoda stops you before. The gesture of entering an amount is the moment of consciousness. There is no shortcut: no one can make you free in your place.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Owoda work?
You enter your salary once. For every expense you're considering, you type the price and Owoda tells you how many hours or days of work it represents. That's it. No tables, no lessons, just the real question asked before you spend.
Is Owoda free?
Yes, price-to-time conversion is free, with a history limited to 7 conversions. The Premium subscription unlocks unlimited history, the analytics dashboard, CSV export, multiple profiles, and includes the Owoda Practical Guide.
How much is Premium?
2,990 CFA per year, or €4.99 / $4.99 depending on your region. A 7-day free trial lets you test before committing. You can cancel anytime from your Google Play or App Store account settings.
Does Owoda manage my money or make investments?
No. Owoda is a calculation and financial education tool. We don't collect your money, we don't invest, we don't lend. All calculations happen on your device, your data stays private.
Does my data leave my phone?
Your financial data (conversions, salary, categories) stays on your device. The app uses the internet only to update exchange rates and manage your Premium subscription. No behavioral tracking, no sharing with third parties. Your finances are your business.
What does Owoda know about me?
Nothing. Owoda does not require an account, email, or phone number. No financial data leaves your device. The app uses the internet only to update exchange rates and manage your Premium subscription through your store.
Will Owoda make me feel guilty?
No. Owoda doesn't judge, it shows. You see what each expense costs you in work time. The rest is your decision. No moralizing notifications, no imposed limits, no goals you didn't set yourself.
Is the Practical Guide in English?
The 17-page guide is written in French. The application itself is available in both French and English.
