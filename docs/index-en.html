How it works

Three steps that will change the way you see every price, forever.

1
Enter your salary

Gross or net, monthly, weekly or hourly. Owoda calculates your real hourly rate, accounting for holidays and commute time.

2
Enter a price

Type any amount — rent, a phone, a meal. Owoda instantly converts it into your currency, even from a foreign currency.

3
See the truth

That smartphone at 450,000 CFA costs nearly 3 months of work. That rent at 75,000 CFA, 10 days. That meal at 1,500 CFA, about an hour and a half. Decide with full knowledge.
