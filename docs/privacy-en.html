Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short
Owoda asks for no account, no name and no email address. Your profile and your history stay on your device. No ads, no audience measurement, no trackers.

1. Data controller

Korvedion SASU, capital €1,000, 40 rue de la Tour d’Auvergne, 44200 Nantes, France, RCS Nantes 132 147 075, publisher of the Owoda app and of the websites owodalabs.com and owoda.app.

Contact for any question about your data: apps@korvedion.com

2. Data that stays on your device

Your profile (income, working hours, commuting time, holidays, currency) and your conversion history are stored only on your phone, in secure storage. Korvedion has no access to them and never receives them. You can delete them at any time from the app’s Settings, or by uninstalling Owoda.

3. Data we process and why

Premium subscription
Purchases go through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), which process payments under their own privacy policies. Korvedion never receives your payment details.
To check your Premium access, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a pseudonymous identifier generated by the app, your purchase history as provided by the stores, and technical information (device type, system and app versions). No name or email address is sent to it.
Legal basis: performance of the subscription contract.
Retention: for the duration of your subscription, then 3 years after it ends, unless you ask us to delete it.

Exchange rate updates
At most once a day, the app queries ExchangeRate-API to get the day’s exchange rates. The request contains no data about you. As with any internet connection, the service receives the device’s IP address.
Legal basis: Korvedion’s legitimate interest in operating the app.

Practical Guide download
The Guide is hosted by Cloudflare. Opening it sends the device’s IP address to Cloudflare, as with any web page.
Legal basis: performance of the subscription contract.

Email
If you write to us, we use your email address, the content of your message and, if you write from the app, the app and system versions added automatically to the message, only to reply to you.
Legal basis: Korvedion’s legitimate interest in answering your requests.
Retention: 3 years after our last exchange.

Visiting owodalabs.com and owoda.app
The websites have no forms, no cookies and no audience measurement tools. Their hosting provider, GitHub, may record technical connection data, including IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

4. Recipients

Your data is never sold or used for advertising. Only Korvedion and the following technical providers can access it, within the limits of their role:

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): subscription management
  • AYR Tech (Pty) Ltd, publisher of ExchangeRate-API: exchange rates
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States): hosting of the Practical Guide
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): hosting of the websites
  • our email service providers

Apple and Google process payments as independent data controllers.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based outside the European Union. Transfers to RevenueCat are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Cloudflare and GitHub apply the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For ExchangeRate-API, only the device’s IP address is sent, at the time of the technical request.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, write to apps@korvedion.com. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

7. Minors

Owoda is intended for people who earn an income. The app is not intended for children under 15.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. In the event of a significant change, the date at the top of this page will be updated and, where necessary, you will be informed in the app.
